How the companion works

The chat history and any details you share live in your browser's localStorage, so a refresh won't wipe the conversation. Clear memory (or /clear) erases all of it.

Without an AI backend configured, Pixel runs on a small built-in offline brain so the page still works. See README.md for wiring up a real provider through the proxy.
